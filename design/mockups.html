Your momentum dropped sharply right after you flagged 😟 frustration at 3–2 in Set 2 — you then lost 5 of 6 points. The same pattern (mistake → rushed points) cost you that set. Settling your routine here is your single biggest lever.
📝Match summary

A hard-fought 3-set win. You controlled Set 1 with aggressive returning, lost focus mid-match, then regrouped to take the deciding tiebreak 10–7.

🧠Mental analysis

Confidence was solid (7/10) but recovery after errors was your weak point. Frustration — your self-reported challenge — lined up exactly with your worst momentum dip.

🎾Technical & tactical

Serve was a weapon (5 aces) but 19 unforced errors — mostly forehands in long rallies — leaked points. Crosscourt rallies favored you; you went down the line too early.
